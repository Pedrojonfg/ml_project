- **Tags:** product | scope

## Context

PHBench was the shortlist backup vs ESA. The proposal is 2 pages with mandatory EDA/prelim; mentioning a second project can dilute focus.

## Options considered

1. **A · No mention** — PDF is ESA-only; backup stays internal. Pros: focus. Cons: no written contingency in the deliverable.
2. **B · One sentence** — brief “if data failed…” Pros: transparency. Cons: reads as indecision.
3. **C · Paragraph** — mini PHBench alternative. Pros: optionality. Cons: steals EDA/method space.

## Decision

Chose **A**: do **not** mention PHBench (or any backup project) in the Project Proposal PDF.

## Why

User selected A. Matches research guidance (primary only in the 2-pager) and the course brief’s content list.

## Consequences

- Proposal narrative, dataset, EDA, methods, next steps = ESA CDMs only.
- PHBench remains an internal reserve in research briefs / this ledger, not in the submission.
