# 024. Limitations / ethics block in the proposal

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Tags:** product | research
- **Decided by:** human

## Context

Course brief does not require a limitations section, but space is tight (2 pages text) and aerospace ops data warrants caveats. Auto-trivial ethics gist already existed in the ledger.

## Options considered

1. **A** — Omit; defer limitations to later work.
2. **B** — Mini-paragraph (3–4 sentences) attached to dataset/methodology.
3. **C** — Dedicated “Limitations” (or “Limitations and ethics”) subsection.
4. **D** — Footnote with DOI/license only.

## Decision

Chose **C**: a short dedicated subsection.

Content must cover, briefly:
- CC BY 4.0 credit to ESA ACT / Space Debris Office (Zenodo release); anonymised historical CDMs.
- Labels are **operational risk estimates**, not observed collisions.
- No live spacecraft commanding / not an ops deployment claim.
- Selection bias: manoeuvre cases removed/truncated in the challenge construction.
- Optional one line: models support analysis/decision-support research only.

## Why

User selected C for a clearer, paper-like honesty signal.

## Consequences

- Budget ~⅓–½ column of page 2 (or a tight block before next steps); protect methodology + next steps (020/022) from being squeezed out — keep Limitations short (≈6–10 lines).
- Do not turn it into a legal essay.
